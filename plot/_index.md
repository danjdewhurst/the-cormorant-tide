# Plot Structure

## Story Structure

**Model:** Three-Act Structure

Act I: Adrian Rusk is found dead on the causeway. Rowan Bell returns to Pelham Hard and discovers the tide board was altered.

Act II: The investigation exposes links between Adrian's research, Theo Sparke's marina scheme, the Mercy Gale, and several local lies.

Act III: Rowan reconstructs the murder, proves Eleanor Sparke killed Adrian, and forces Pelham Hard to confront the older crime.
