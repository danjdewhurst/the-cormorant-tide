# Rowan's Return

Rowan Bell begins the book determined to treat Pelham Hard as jurisdiction, not home. The case forces her to see how silence operates in a place where everyone knows one another. By the end, she does not forgive the town wholesale, but she stops mistaking distance for freedom.
