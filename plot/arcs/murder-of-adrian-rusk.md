# Murder of Adrian Rusk

Adrian Rusk is lured to St Osyth's Eye after finding the missing harbour log from the Mercy Gale. Eleanor Sparke alters the tide board, meets him near Cormorant House, strikes him with her silver-topped stick, and leaves him unconscious where the rising water will make the death look like an accident.
