# Mercy Gale Truth

The accepted story of the Mercy Gale blamed Eddie Lanyon for cutting a towline and praised Christopher Sparke for attempting a rescue. The true sequence is that Christopher Sparke's launch struck the lifeboat, Eleanor Sparke lied, and local witnesses accepted the lie because it spared them money, shame, and conflict.
