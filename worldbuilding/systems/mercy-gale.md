# Mercy Gale

The Mercy Gale was a storm in November 1998 during which Pelham Hard's lifeboat went out to assist a private launch and came back damaged, with two crew dead and one young volunteer, Eddie Lanyon, blamed for cutting a towline. The official account made heroes of the Sparke family and a scapegoat of Eddie.

The truth is that Christopher Sparke's launch struck the lifeboat after returning from an illegal salvage run. Eleanor Sparke lied at the inquest to protect him.
