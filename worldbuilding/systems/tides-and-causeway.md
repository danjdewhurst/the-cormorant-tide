# Tides and Causeway

The murder depends on local tide knowledge. The causeway to St Osyth's Eye is covered quickly on a spring tide. The harbour tide board is trusted by residents and visitors, and its alteration creates both the means of luring Adrian Rusk into danger and a clue pointing towards someone with access.
