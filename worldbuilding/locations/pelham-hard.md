# Pelham Hard

Pelham Hard is compact enough for rumour to move faster than official news. The harbour smells of diesel, bladderwrack, frying oil, wet rope, and old stone. Above it, cottages climb towards the church and the chalk path. Below it, the tide exposes mud, chains, pottery, and the long ribs of forgotten boats.
