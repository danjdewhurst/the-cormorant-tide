# St Osyth's Eye

St Osyth's Eye is a low tidal island reached by a stone causeway at low water. It holds a ruined quarantine house called Cormorant House, a cracked boathouse, salt-bitten grass, and a colony of cormorants that blacken the roofline at dusk.

The causeway is passable only during a narrow window. Locals know this. Visitors learn it from the warning board or from fear.
