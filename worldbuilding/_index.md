# Worldbuilding

## World Overview

Pelham Hard is a fictional Sussex harbour town built around a working quay, a shingle beach, chalk cliffs, and the tidal causeway to St Osyth's Eye. It survives on fishing, summer visitors, small galleries, weathered money, and committees that can make a feud last thirty years.
