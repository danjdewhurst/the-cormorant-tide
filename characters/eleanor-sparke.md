# Eleanor Sparke

Eleanor Sparke is the town's most polished benefactor: widow, committee chair, patron of the lifeboat fund, and keeper of Pelham Hard's approved history. She murdered Adrian Rusk after learning he had found evidence that her late husband caused the Mercy Gale and let another man take the blame.

Eleanor's power is social rather than physical. She knows who owes whom, who fears embarrassment, and how to turn gratitude into silence.
