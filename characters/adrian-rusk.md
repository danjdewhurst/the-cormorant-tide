# Adrian Rusk

Dr Adrian Rusk is a retired historian who moved to Pelham Hard twelve years before the novel begins. Clever, lonely, and sharp-edged, he has been researching the Mercy Gale, a celebrated local lifeboat disaster from 1998. He is found dead on the tidal causeway to St Osyth's Eye after discovering proof that the official version of the old disaster was false.
