# Rowan Bell

Detective Inspector Rowan Bell is a Sussex Police detective in her late thirties, recently assigned back near her home town after years in Brighton and London units. She is observant, dry, and reluctant to trade on local knowledge, partly because Pelham Hard still treats her as Max Bell's daughter before it treats her as police.

Rowan's emotional arc is about returning without surrendering. She begins by mistrusting the town's appetite for old stories, then learns that the old stories are the crime scene.
