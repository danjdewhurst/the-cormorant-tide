# Mara Fenwick

Mara Fenwick is Pelham Hard's harbourmaster and Rowan's old school friend. Competent, guarded, and physically at ease on the water, she becomes a credible suspect because the tide board was altered with a harbour key and because she lies about where she was on the night Adrian Rusk died.

Her lie protects her father from shame, not the killer from justice.
