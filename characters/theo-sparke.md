# Theo Sparke

Theo Sparke is a property developer trying to convert Pelham Hard's derelict sail lofts and old quarantine house into a luxury marina scheme. He is vain, under pressure, and willing to bend planning rules, which makes him an obvious suspect, but he did not kill Adrian Rusk.
