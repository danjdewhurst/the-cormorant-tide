# Nessa Quill

Nessa Quill runs the Pelham Hard Museum archive. She is younger than most of the heritage committee, serious about records, and quietly furious that local history is treated as decoration until it becomes inconvenient.

Nessa helped Adrian Rusk with the Mercy Gale research, but held back one document because she feared what it would do to people still living.
